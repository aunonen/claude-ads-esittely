# Ad Design Guide — HTML statics from the client's real photos

How `creative-generation` turns a brief card into an ad. Every ad is a small
HTML page laid out with CSS on top of the client's **own photos**, rendered to
PNG with `render.py`. No image is generated by AI.

Files in this folder:

| File        | What                                                                                                                                                    |
| ----------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `base.css`  | Shared layout primitives (frame sizes, photo, scrims, headline, badges, USP row, split, card, before/after) and the creative concept patterns (chat, note, search, news, checklist, sticky, notification, calendar, comparison, bars, poll). `render.py` copies it next to each design. |
| `render.py` | HTML → PNG for SQ (1080×1080), VT (1080×1920), HZ (1920×1080) with headless Chrome/Edge.                                                                |

Per client: `reference/brand/brand.css` holds the brand colours and fonts as CSS
variables (`--brand-primary`, `--brand-secondary`, `--brand-bg`,
`--brand-text`, `--font-headline`, `--font-body`, optional
`--headline-weight`) and loads the fonts (Google Fonts `@import`, or
`@font-face` pointing at files in `reference/brand/fonts/`). Brand values
always come from there, never hardcoded in a design.

---

## The authenticity rules (why the ads don't look AI-made)

The user's clients need ads that look like a real business made them. This is
the most important section.

1. **Real photos only.** Every photo in an ad comes from the client's
   `reference/products/Images/` (their projects, team, premises, products).
   Never generate, upscale-with-AI, retouch-with-AI, extend or composite a
   photo, and never use stock imagery. If a card needs a photo the client
   doesn't have, stop and tell the user which photo is missing instead of
   working around it.
2. **Only honest photo handling:** crop (`object-fit: cover` +
   `object-position`), and a subtle dark scrim behind text for legibility.
   Nothing that changes what the photo shows.
3. **Restraint over decoration.** No glossy gradients, glows, glassmorphism,
   3D effects, sparkles, emoji on the image, or "AI-poster" symmetry. Plenty
   of the photo visible.
4. **Plain, specific language.** Headlines sound like the business owner
   talking, in the client's language (Finnish by default per the agency
   `CLAUDE.md`). Concrete over clever: "Kylpyhuone valmiina kahdessa
   viikossa" beats "Koe kylpyhuoneesi uusi taso". How the text is written is
   set in `creative-strategy`'s _Creative Copy_ section; this guide only lays it out.
5. **Native formats are welcome.** Formats that look like something people
   see every day often perform best and look least like ads: a customer
   review card over the project photo, a before/after pair, a simple
   text-on-photo, a handwritten-style note, a plain checklist.
6. **Check the result like a sceptic.** Before showing a render, ask: would a
   local customer believe a real company posted this? If anything looks
   templated or synthetic, simplify.

These ads carry no AI-generated imagery and no AI metadata, so they give Meta
no basis for an "AI info" label. Keep it that way: don't put AI-generated
images into `reference/products/Images/`, and keep Meta's creative
enhancements off at campaign setup.

## Design file anatomy

One HTML file per creative, holding all three sizes. Save it as
`outputs/generated-assets/designs/<CreativeName>.html` (no size or date in the
name; `render.py` adds them). Paths are relative to that folder.

```html
<!doctype html>
<html data-size="sq">
  <head>
    <meta charset="utf-8" />
    <script>
      document.documentElement.dataset.size = location.hash.slice(1) || "sq";
    </script>
    <link rel="stylesheet" href="base.css" />
    <link rel="stylesheet" href="../../../reference/brand/brand.css" />
    <style>
      /* creative-specific tweaks only, using brand variables */
    </style>
  </head>
  <body>
    <div class="ad">
      <img
        class="photo"
        src="../../../reference/products/Images/<folder>/<file>"
        style="--pos-vt: 35% 50%"
      />
      <div class="scrim-bottom"></div>
      <img class="logo" src="../../../reference/design-assets/<logo file>" />
      <!-- only if the card's Logo: is Yes -->
      <div class="copy bottom on-photo">
        <div class="headline">Exact headline from the brief</div>
        <div class="sub">Exact subline from the brief</div>
        <div class="badge-slot">
          <span class="badge pill-light"
            ><span class="stars">★★★★★</span> exact proof wording</span
          >
        </div>
      </div>
    </div>
  </body>
</html>
```

- `base.css` is linked **before** `brand.css` so brand values override the defaults.
- Size switches: `html[data-size="sq|vt|hz"]` selectors in the `<style>`
  block, plus the helper classes `only-sq`/`only-vt`/`only-hz` and
  `not-sq`/`not-vt`/`not-hz` when a size needs a different arrangement
  (typical for HZ: copy on the left half with `scrim-left`).
- Photo crop per size: `--pos`, `--pos-sq`, `--pos-vt`, `--pos-hz` on `.photo`
  (CSS `object-position`, e.g. `30% 50%`). Re-crop instead of squeezing.
- Copy text is the brief card's wording **exactly**. Never invent a claim,
  number, rating, guarantee or badge.

Render:

```bash
python ~/.claude/skills/ad-design/render.py outputs/generated-assets/designs/<CreativeName>.html
```

Writes `outputs/generated-assets/<CreativeName>-SQ_<DD-MM-YYYY>.png` (and VT,
HZ) and prints `OK`/`FAILED` per size. Several design files can be passed at
once. `--sizes vt` re-renders just one size.

## Layout patterns

Pick per card from the brief's `Layout` and `Visual direction`. Rotate across
the batch so a batch doesn't look like one template.

| Pattern              | Build                                                                                        | Good for                                                     |
| -------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Photo + headline** | `.photo` + `.scrim-bottom` + `.copy.bottom.on-photo`                                         | Results, finished projects, the default                      |
| **Headline on top**  | `.photo` + `.scrim-top` + `.copy.top.on-photo` (logo then sits below the copy or is omitted) | Photos whose subject is in the lower half                    |
| **Split**            | `.split` with `.side-photo` (photo inside) and `.side-copy` on `--brand-secondary`           | Offers, USP lists, copy-heavy cards                          |
| **Review card**      | `.photo` (project) + `.card` with `.stars`, `.quote`, `.who`                                 | Testimonials (exact quote + name/initials from context only) |
| **Before / after**   | `.ba` with two `<figure>`s, each a `.photo` + `<figcaption>Ennen</figcaption>` / `Jälkeen`   | Renovation, cleaning, installation, any visible change       |
| **Plain text**       | `.ad` on `--brand-bg` or `--brand-secondary`, large `.headline`, small photo or none         | Myth-busting, questions, price/offer statements              |

### Creative concept patterns

When a card's `Creative concept` is a native format, build it with these
`base.css` classes. Put the element inside `.native` (vertically centred;
`.native.top` sits under the logo; in HZ it moves to the right half) over a
`.photo` or a plain `--brand-bg`. Every format is a **picture** of the thing,
never working UI (see the rules below).

| Creative concept                    | Build                                                                                                                                  |
| ----------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Whatsapp/Slack convo                | `.chat` with `.chat-head` (client name + logo), `.bubble.in` / `.bubble.out`, optional `.time`. `--chat-out` sets the outgoing colour |
| iphone notes                        | `.note` with `.note-date`, `.note-title`, then `<p>` or a `.checklist`                                                                 |
| Google Search                       | `.search-bar` (the query) + `.search-result` with `.site`, `.title`, snippet. The client's own result, no search-engine logo           |
| news / breaking news / newspaper front page | `.news` with `.masthead` (client name or a section word, never a real paper), `.kicker`, `.news-headline`, `.news-photo` > `.photo` |
| checklist                           | `.checklist` (`<li>` = done ✓, `<li class="open">` = still to do –)                                                                     |
| sticky note with text               | `.sticky` (slightly rotated yellow note)                                                                                               |
| handwritten text on paper / whiteboard text | `.paper` (lined paper). Uses `--font-hand` if `brand.css` defines one                                                           |
| phone notification                  | `.notif` with the client's logo as `<img>`, `.notif-app` (name + "nyt"), `.notif-title`, `.notif-text`. One card, no counter          |
| calendar reminder                   | `.calendar` with `.cal-date` (`.cal-month`, `.cal-day`), `.cal-title`, `.cal-when`                                                     |
| spreadsheet / choose A/b / comparison | `<table class="compare">`, the client's column `class="ours"`                                                                        |
| data visualization (charts etc.)    | `.bars` > `.bar` (label + `.bar-track` > `.bar-fill` with `style="--v: 70%"`); `.bar.muted` for the alternative                        |
| ig poll                             | `.poll` with `.poll-q` and `.poll-opt` (`style="--v: 64%"`), shown as **results with percentages**, never as an unanswered sticker  |
| Review/trustpilot, Testimonial card | The **Review card** layout above (`.card`); no review-platform logo                                                                    |
| before/after                        | The **Before / after** layout above                                                                                                    |
| bg photo + text                     | **Photo + headline** or **Headline on top** above                                                                                     |
| IG story, POV, screenshot, Email screenshot, Reddit/X/Linkedin post, internet meme, calculator | No ready class: compose from `.card`, `.note` and `.native` in the design's `<style>`, same rules |

**Rules for every native format** (Meta rejects "non-existent functionality",
and the ad must not mislead):

- No play or close buttons, unread counters, red notification dots, empty
  checkboxes, "swipe up" arrows or anything else that looks tappable.
- No real platform's, app's or publication's name or logo. The client's own
  name and logo go where an app name or masthead would be.
- Messages, reviews and quotes use only real wording from `context/` (Social
  Proof Assets, verbatim customer quotes). Numbers in tables and charts come
  from `context/` too, never invented to make a point.
- The client must be recognisable as the advertiser: its name or logo in the
  ad, or in the first line of the primary text.

Size adaptation:

- **SQ** is the reference composition.
- **VT (Stories/Reels):** keep the bottom ~460px clear. `.copy.bottom` already
  sits above it via `--safe-bottom`. Use the extra height for the photo, not
  for more text.
- **HZ:** copy on one side (usually left, `.copy.center.left-half` +
  `.scrim-left`), the photo's subject on the other. Shift `--pos-hz` so the
  subject isn't behind the text.

## Agency style library

`../../library/style/style-guide.md` (agency hub) holds visual traits seen in
proven ads across several advertisers (`S-VIS-*`). Supported and Proven entries
are optional guidance when choosing a layout, crop or text position. The
authenticity rules above always win; never copy a reference ad's look.

## Typography

- **Headline font** (`--font-headline`): the headline only.
- **Body font** (`--font-body`): everything else (subline, badge, USPs, card text).
- Never a third font, except an optional handwriting font (`--font-hand` in
  `brand.css`) for the sticky-note and handwritten formats. No all-caps
  headlines unless the brand does that.
- Keep numbers and units together with a non-breaking space: `60&nbsp;%`,
  `15&nbsp;€`, `12&nbsp;900&nbsp;€`, so a line never breaks between them.
- Text lengths are set when the card is written (`creative-strategy`'s
  _Creative Copy_ section). Here the wording is fixed: if a line wraps to more
  than 3 lines in SQ, reduce `--headline-size` for that design rather than
  cutting words, and tell the user if it still doesn't fit.
- Make sure Finnish characters (ä, ö, å) render in the chosen fonts.

## Logo

- When the card's `Logo:` is Yes (~3 in 4 cards across the pool; inside a test every ad follows the control): the primary logo lockup
  from `CLAUDE.md`'s Brand Assets, `.logo` (centred at the top) or `.logo.left`
  for native formats. Clear space around it, never over a busy part of the photo.
- When No: no logo element at all.
- Use a light or dark logo variant, whichever reads on that photo, if both
  exist in `reference/design-assets/`.

## Social proof badge (~half the pool)

Only the exact wording from the offer brief's Social Proof Assets. A challenger
uses exactly the control's badge and treatment. Across the pool, rotate the
treatment: `.badge.pill`, `.badge.pill-light`,
`.badge.plain`, `.badge.seal`, or a `.card` review. Star ratings use the
`.stars` element (★★★★★) and only with a real rating from context. One badge
per ad, at one position.

## USP row

`.usp-row` with 3–4 short benefits from the offer brief (`<span>` each, the
check mark is automatic). Stacks vertically in VT. Never a single lone item.
If `reference/design-assets/` has icon files the card names, use `<img>` icons
of one shared colour variant instead of the check marks.

## Review checklist (each design, before showing it)

1. Look at all three PNGs with `Read`, not just the SQ.
2. Photo is the client's own, uncropped where it matters (faces, the work itself).
3. Headline/subline/badge match the brief exactly. No invented claims.
4. Text is legible on the photo (add/strengthen the scrim or move the copy if not).
5. Nothing overlaps: logo, headline, badge, card all have breathing room.
6. VT: no key element in the bottom ~460px.
7. HZ: the subject isn't hidden behind the copy.
8. Fonts rendered (no fallback serif/sans), and ä/ö look right.
9. Logo present only when the card says Yes.
10. The sceptic test: would a local customer believe a real company posted this?
11. Native formats: nothing looks tappable, no real platform's or paper's name
    or logo, the client is recognisable as the advertiser.
