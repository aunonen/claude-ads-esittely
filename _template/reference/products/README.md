# Client Photos

The client's own photos go here, with **one folder per product or service** (for a service business: per service or project type). These photos are the raw material of every ad: the ads are designed on top of them, and **nothing is AI-generated**. Only photos in these folders can be used in ads.

```
reference/products/Images/
├── kylpyhuoneremontti/
│   ├── kylpyhuone-espoo-ennen.jpg
│   ├── kylpyhuone-espoo-jalkeen.jpg
│   └── tiimi-tyomaalla.jpg
├── lampopumput/
│   └── ...
└── yritys/                 # team, vehicles, premises, the owner
    └── ...
```

For an online store it's the same idea: one folder per product, with packshots and in-use photos.

## What makes a good ad photo

- **Real and specific:** finished work, before/after pairs shot from the same angle, the team on site, happy customers (with permission).
- **Large:** at least 1500 px on the short side, so it can be cropped to square, vertical and horizontal.
- **Room for text:** photos with a calm area (sky, wall, floor) are easiest to put a headline on.
- **Descriptive filenames:** say what the photo shows (`-ennen` / `-jalkeen` for before/after pairs). The skills choose photos by looking at them and by their names.

Claims (prices, guarantees, certifications) **don't** go here. They belong in `context/offer-brief.md` and `context/business-info.md`, and the skills only use claims from those files.
