# Research Sources — {{BRAND_NAME}}

> Every source the research (`customer-deep-research-report.md`, `audience-hypotheses.md`) is built on. Written by the `customer-research` skill. Lets anyone see how solid a finding is, and what to read next. No names or contact details of private people.

**Source types, strongest first:** `own customer` (the client's own customers' words: their reviews of the client, messages or questions read out in the client interview, customer stories on its site) · `client interview` (what the client and staff say customers ask, fear and decide on, incl. the onboarding) · `public review` (reviews of competitors and other providers) · `customer story` (media and case-study stories where a customer speaks) · `forum` (Suomi24, vauva.fi, trade forums, Facebook groups, Reddit…) · `media` (news, authorities, guides) · `search` (autocomplete, "People also ask")
